How to Submit Custom Measurements

When selecting a piece, choose "Custom Measurements" from the dropdown menu and specify your exact tailored dimensions (e.g., Bust, Waist, Hips, Sleeve Length, and Shoulder width) in the instruction box. Our master tailors will handcraft your garment explicitly to your profile.
