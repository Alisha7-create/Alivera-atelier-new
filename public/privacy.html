At Alivéra Atelier ("we," "our," or "us"), we respect your privacy and are committed to protecting the personal information you share with us. This Privacy Policy outlines how we collect, use, process, and safeguard your data when you visit our website, order custom apparel, use our mobile application, or interact with our authentication services.

By accessing or using aliveraatelier.in, you agree to the practices described in this Privacy Policy.

1. Information We Collect

We collect information directly from you, automatically via your browser or device, and through third-party authentication services.

A. Personal Information You Provide Directly

  • Account & Contact Data: Name, email address, phone number, and shipping/billing addresses when you register an account or place an order.
  • Customization & Bespoke Data: Measurements, custom design specifications, style preferences, and special order notes.
  • Customer Support: Correspondence, feedback, and inquiry records when you contact our atelier team.

B. Information Collected Automatically

  • Device & Usage Data: IP address, browser type, operating system, device identifiers, referring URLs, and page navigation patterns collected via logs and analytics.
  • Cookies & Tracking: Session identification cookies and performance tracking necessary for cart functionality and secure session management.

2. Google OAuth & Third-Party Authentication

To provide a seamless and secure login experience across our website and mobile application, we offer single sign-on via Google OAuth 2.0 (Google Identity Services).

  • Data Received from Google: When you log in using your Google account, we request permission to access basic profile details, specifically your full name, primary email address, and profile picture URL.
  • Usage of OAuth Data: We use this information solely to authenticate your identity, create your Alivéra Atelier user account, and maintain a secure logged-in session.
  • Data Storage: Google authentication tokens are processed securely on our edge servers (aliveraatelier.in) to issue native session identifiers. We do not store or access your Google password.
  • No Sharing of Auth Data: We do not share, sell, or monetize your Google OAuth profile data with third-party advertisers or data brokers.

3. How We Use Your Information

We process your data for legitimate business purposes and to fulfill our contractual commitments to you:

  1. Order Fulfillment: Processing orders, custom tailoring, shipping bespoke garments, and providing delivery updates.
  2. Account Management: Maintaining your profile, saved measurements, order history, and security preferences.
  3. Customer Service: Resolving sizing queries, alterations, order tracking, and general support.
  4. Platform Security & Optimization: Preventing fraudulent transactions, securing API routes, monitoring system stability, and optimizing site performance.
  5. Marketing Communications: Delivering tailored collection updates, exclusive previews, or promotional offers (only where you have opted in).

4. Payments & Order Fulfillment

  • Payment Processing: We do not store or process complete credit/debit card numbers or UPI PINs on our servers. All payments are processed through PCI-DSS compliant third-party payment gateways.
  • Logistics & Delivery: Necessary contact details (name, shipping address, contact phone number) are securely transmitted to authorized courier and logistics partners exclusively for delivering your orders.
